# Commercial use

openFPS is free for noncommercial use. Commercial use needs a separate licence from carpetshark.

| Part | Licence |
|---|---|
| Code, recipes, simulations and their data (`*.py`, `*.js`, `*.json`, `*.html`, `*.sh`, workflows) | [PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](LICENSE.md) |
| Documentation, notes and images (`*.md`, plots, renders) | [CC BY-NC 4.0](LICENSE-DOCS.txt), attribution "carpetshark" |

Noncommercial covers personal study, hobby projects, research, teaching and use by charities and public
institutions, as each licence defines it. Anything else, such as shipping an asset in a paid or ad-supported game,
using it in client work, or selling a derivative, is commercial.

## Getting a commercial licence

Commercial licences are available on request. Open an issue at
https://github.com/evanrobinson2/openFPS/issues titled "Commercial licence", describe the asset and how you would
use it, and carpetshark will follow up. Don't post anything confidential in the issue.

Product names are trademarks of their owners. Neither licence grants any trademark rights, and openFPS is not
affiliated with any manufacturer.
