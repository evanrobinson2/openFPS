Generic QD 5.56 mm suppressor on the HK416: simulation sample

Everything here is computed in the page. sim/suppressor-qd-556.js runs the gas model of the shot (bore, blast chamber, six baffles, end cap) and turns it into a patch for the HK416 sound tables, an input for the HK416 action, can heating and flash scales. The sound is synthesised by hk416/sound/hk416-sound.js from the patched tables; nothing is a recording. Credit: carpetshark.
