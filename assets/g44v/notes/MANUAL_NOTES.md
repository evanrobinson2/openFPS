# G44 V: facts used from the instructions for use

Source: the maker's "Instructions for use", G44 V, edition 03-26, English, 48 pages (local copy only, see `REFERENCES.md`).
These are summaries of facts with page numbers, not quotations.

## What the pistol is
- A blowback pistol in .22 l.r. with the maker's striker ("Safe Action") fire control; the manual's pictures and drawings are of this model (p. 6).
- Polymer frame with hardened steel guides; slide stop lever and magazine catch worked by the shooting hand; interchangeable beavertail backstraps; extended, reversible magazine catch (p. 6).
- Slide: steel guide rails carrying the serial number, and a matte black polymer slide insert that saves weight (p. 8). Barrel: cold-hammer forged, rounded interior profile (p. 8).
- Loaded chamber indicator: an opening at the rear of the barrel hood through which a chambered cartridge can be seen (p. 8, pictures 1-3).
- Three automatic safeties: a trigger safety lever in the trigger that blocks rearward movement until pressed; a firing pin safety that blocks the firing pin until the trigger bar pushes it up; a drop safety, the trigger bar resting on a ramp in the trigger mechanism housing and holding the rear of the firing pin until it is lowered (p. 7).
- No manual safety (pp. 1, 7). Works from -40 to 50 degrees C (p. 6).

## Named parts
- Nomenclature (p. 9): magazine, beavertail backstrap, trigger housing pin, calibre marking, loaded chamber indicator, trigger, mounting rail, slide lock, trigger safety, trigger guard, magazine catch, front sight, rear sight, slide stop lever, slide, muzzle (barrel), frame.
- Component list (p. 36) and exploded drawing (p. 37): items 1 to 35 and 48, 49. Items 25 and 30 are blank. The list is reproduced, by item number, in `DECOMPOSITION.md`.
- The set includes a screwdriver for the adjustable rear sight and a beavertail backstrap set (p. 10).

## Operation
- Loading: the magazine has loading assist tabs on both sides to pull the follower down (p. 11, pictures 4-5). The slide is pulled fully back and released, or released with the slide stop lever (p. 12).
- Ammunition: .22 l.r. made to SAAMI or CIP specifications only; some .22 l.r. loads may not have the energy to cycle the slide fully, and ammunition should be tested (p. 12).
- The slide locks open on an empty magazine (p. 34, lock-open test). The slide stop lever should snap down when lifted and released (p. 31).
- After a dry fire the trigger stays back until the slide is cycled (p. 33, trigger reset test).
- Hearing protection of at least NRR 24 dB is called for (p. 13). A bullet may travel about 3 km (p. 13, a general warning).

## Field strip (pp. 21-23)
Magazine out, slide back to clear and check the chamber, slide forward, trigger pulled (the trigger must be to the rear to remove the slide). Slide drawn back about 3 mm (1/8 in) and held; slide lock pulled down on both sides; slide pushed forward off the frame. Recoil spring assembly pressed forward and lifted out. Barrel pushed forward at least 1 cm, lifted, and drawn out to the rear. The slide plate and the polymer part of the slide are not to be removed (p. 23).

## Reassembly and checks (pp. 30-34)
- Barrel in, recoil spring assembly with its small end in the slide's front recess and its large end in the semicircular notch of the barrel lugs (p. 32).
- Firing pin safety test: the firing pin lug pressed forward with about 23 to 36 N must not pass the firing pin safety (p. 30). The firing pin is held forward by spring pressure when the slide is off (p. 30).
- The ejector is part of the trigger mechanism housing in the frame (p. 31). The extractor claw sits at the breech face (pp. 27, 30).

## Backstraps and magazine catch (pp. 38-40)
- Medium backstrap adds 2 mm, large adds 4 mm; both carry a beavertail. They hook at the bottom rear of the frame and are held by the (longer) trigger housing pin (pp. 38-39).
- The magazine catch is held by a straight wire spring reached through the magazine well and can be fitted for either hand (p. 40).
