Gloved hands on the HK416: sample

Everything drawn here comes from hands-gloved.js reading hands-gloved.json: the skeleton of both hands is posed by the module's forward kinematics, frame by frame, in the rifle's frame. The grey boxes are the bounding boxes of a few rifle parts, for orientation only; the glove surface itself is built by model/build.py and is not drawn here. Drag the picture to turn it.
