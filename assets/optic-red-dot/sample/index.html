Generic 2 MOA red-dot sight: simulation sample

The sight picture, drawn from optics/optic-red-dot.js. The dot's direction comes from the ray trace of the sight's collimator, so it drifts off the target as your eye leaves the optical axis (parallax), by an amount that depends on how far away the target is. Its brightness is a luminance set by the switch; how it looks depends on the scene behind it. Shots are flown by the rifle's ballistics/hk416-ballistics.js with the sight mounted. Drag on the picture to move your eye. The small picture is the middle of the big one, enlarged. On the big picture the dot is drawn at least 3 pixels wide so it can be seen; its true size is in the small one.
