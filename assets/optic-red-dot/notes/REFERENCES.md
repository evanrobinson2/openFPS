# References: generic red-dot sight

Nothing listed here is in the repository. Figures were read on 2026-11-17 unless marked recalled.

## Published figures used

| Source | What was taken from it | How it was read |
|---|---|---|
| Aimpoint, CompM4 / CompM4s user manual, section 1.1 "Specification" | Class figures for a 30 mm tube red dot: length 135 mm, width 51 mm, height 57 mm, weight 370 g; 1x; eye relief unlimited; dot 2 MOA; 16 brightness positions (7 for night vision, 8 daylight, 1 extra bright); adjustment ±2 m at 100 m; 1 click = 16 mm at 100 m; band-pass and red-reflecting coatings | The manual's PDF (retailer copy), read locally and not kept |
| Aimpoint CompM4 product sheet | "Dot visible against a background luminance of 0.1 to 55,000 lx" | A web search summary only; I did not open the sheet |
| Green Eye Tactical, "Comparative Study of Red Dot Sight Parallax" (2017) | Average dot deviation over all distances and testers: 4.5 MOA (Micro T-2), 6.29 MOA (CompM2), 9.68 MOA (Micro T-1) | A transcription in a post on the accuratereloading.com optics forum (thread 7031016252), via a search summary; not the study itself |
| Stanley and Davies, "The effect of field of view size on steady-state pupil diameter", Ophthalmic and Physiological Optics, 1995 | Pupil diameter against luminance for a 60° field | Recalled formula |
| Wyman, Sloan and Shirley, "Simple Analytic Approximations to the CIE XYZ Color Matching Functions", JCGT, 2013 | Analytic colour matching functions for the window tint | Recalled formula |
| Mount height of AR-height 30 mm red-dot mounts | 39 mm optical axis over the rail | Recalled; not found in today's searches |
| `assets/hk416/model/build.py`, `assets/hk416/ballistics/ballistics.json` | Rail section, lug pitch, socket positions (measured from a local build); trajectories | This repository |
