Grenades: simulation sample

Everything on this page is computed from the asset's simulations: the flight, bounces and fuse come from throw/grenades-throw.js, the damage, flash and smoke from effects/grenades-effects.js, and every sound is synthesised by sound/grenades-sound.js. No recordings, no mesh files. This is a game model: what a player sees, hears and feels, from published figures. Nothing plays until you press Throw; start with the volume low.
