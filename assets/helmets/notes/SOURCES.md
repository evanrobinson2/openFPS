# Helmet kit: sources

Every number in `model/presets.json` comes from one of these, or is marked as an inference below. Documents are cited,
not committed (rule 6). `model/check.py` tests the built shells against the published figures; results are in
`checks.json`.

## Inferences (stated as such)

- **Shell shape between the measured extents** (superellipse planform and dome exponents, egg factor, crown offset):
  drawn from general knowledge of these helmets, then scaled so the exterior equals S1 (US) exactly. No official
  side or top drawing was reachable from the cloud, so the curvature is not drawing-checked.
- **Cut lines** (depth of the rim below the crown by azimuth): high cut for the US and contractor shells, mid cut for
  the Eastern shell. The contractor cut was tuned so the shell's outer area meets S4's 994 cm² (it gives 1021.6 cm²,
  +2.8 %, inside the ±3 %).
- **US shell thickness 7.6 mm** (ACH class); **Eastern 6.8 mm**, chosen so the shell's mass at an aramid laminate
  density of 1.25 g/cm³ meets S7's "no more than 1 kg" (986 g).
- **Contractor size** (262 x 236 x 170 mm) and **Eastern size** (272 x 242 x 182 mm): inferred, close to S1's L.
- Laminate densities in `check.py`: aramid/phenolic 1.30, UHMWPE with paint and edge band 1.10, aramid 1.25 g/cm³.
- **Accessories** (rail section 22 x 7.6 mm, shroud plate 72 x 44 mm, one-hole receiver opening 17 x 9.5 mm, bungee
  4 mm, loop panels, counterweight pouch, headset cups 86 x 80 x 36 mm, chin cup): sized from the class of part,
  not from a maker's drawing. Names are generic (rule 2: no makers' names on parts).
- **Head landmarks** for the worn strap pose (chin, jaw, nape relative to the top of the head): adult male averages,
  inferred; the `hang` pose is for display on a stand.
- **Camouflage**: an original four-colour digital pattern generated by `textures.py`, not a copy of any issued pattern.

## Grade

By Evan's scale this is closest to **B** for the US and contractor shells (official dimensions and published
figures, shape from knowledge of real helmets) and **C** for the Eastern shell (only a published weight). Evan
assigns grades; this note does not.
